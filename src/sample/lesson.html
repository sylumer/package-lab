Connecting to the LMS…

1. Fire needs three things

Heat, fuel and oxygen. Take any one away and the fire goes out.

2. Raise the alarm

If you discover a fire, raise the alarm first, then leave by the nearest safe exit.

3. Extinguishers

Water extinguishers are for paper and wood. Never use water on electrical or fat fires.
